Course Structure

Detailed semester-wise syllabus breakdown and course pathways for B.Tech in Chemical Engineering at BIT Sindri.

Semester I

Covers fundamental engineering mathematics, physics, engineering graphics, basic electrical systems, and foundational laboratory practice.

Open Full Resolution Diagram

Semester II

Covers advanced engineering mathematics, general engineering chemistry, programming in C, workshop fundamentals, and communication skills.

Open Full Resolution Diagram

Semester III

Introduces core department subjects including Fluid Mechanics, Material and Energy Balance Calculations, and Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics-I.

Open Full Resolution Diagram

Semester IV

Focuses on Heat Transfer Operations, Mechanical Operations & Particle Technology, Thermodynamics-II, and Numerical Analysis Methods.

Open Full Resolution Diagram

Semester V

In-depth coverage of Mass Transfer Operations-I (Distillation, Absorption), Chemical Reaction Engineering-I, and Inorganic Chemical Technology.

Open Full Resolution Diagram

Semester VI

Advanced Mass Transfer-II (Extraction, Drying, Membrane separation), Process Dynamics & Instrumentation Control, and Petroleum Refinery Engineering.

Open Full Resolution Diagram

Semester VII

Plant Design and Economics, Industrial Safety & Hazardous Waste Mitigation, Biochemical Engineering, and Departmental Electives.

Open Full Resolution Diagram

Semester VIII

Major Capstone Project, Comprehensive Viva-Voce, Industrial In-Plant Training Seminars, and Advanced Polymer Science.
