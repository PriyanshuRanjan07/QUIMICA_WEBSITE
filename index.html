About The Society

Legacy of Innovation

Official Chemical Engineering Society

QUIMICA is the official branch society of the Chemical Engineering Department, which also hosts an annual technical colloquium.

Founded by ambitious students, the organization has since grown to include professors, alumni, and industry professionals.

Its significant events in the past were Innoverse, SmartScape, ElementaX, Jeopardy, Shaastra, Tech Kriti, Advaita, and various career seminars.

Office Location: MC 31, Chemical Department Building, BIT Sindri.
